# Changelog

## 4.3.0

- camelCase, kebabCase, snakeCase and slugify, for the CMS (identifiers,
  CSS classes and article URLs).
- ellipsize: like truncate, but counts what people see as characters and
  prefers to cut at a space; for cards and notifications.
- formatSize: decimal units, for network and disk figures. formatBytes
  stays binary.
- percent, for the dashboards.

## 4.2.0

- relativeDays.
